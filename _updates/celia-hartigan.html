Personal Introduction

I am a registered nurse and the clinical research manager in the Department of Dermatology at UMass Chan. I’ve been a nurse for over 40 years and have been working in research for about 31 years.

Your Background and Journey

Thirty years ago, the field of research nursing was just beginning, but now clinical research nursing is recognized by the American Nurses Association as a nursing specialty. I enjoy the balance of providing care for the research participant along with the commitment to the research protocol. Before joining the Dermatology Department in 2019, I was a research nurse in the Diabetes Division at UMass Chan. One of the highlights of my time in the Diabetes Division was to participate in a cutting edge islet transplantation trial for patients with diabetes. Today, I’m excited to be part of the VIGOR research group and to have the opportunity to work with such a large group of study participants over the coming years. Being a part of an excellent team that includes Drs. Harris and Garber for the study of vitiligo is rewarding. Their passion and commitment for vitiligo is contagious and evident throughout the whole research team from bench to bedside.

Outside of Work

Outside of work, my husband is retired, and we have two adult children and two dogs. Our daughter and her dog just moved back from Colorado so there are three dogs in the house right now. Our son lives in Cambridge, MA with his dog, so you can say we’re a dog-loving family! My favorite activity is traveling to new places. Over the summer, I went on a trip to Egypt with my sister, and I just returned home from a transatlantic cruise that sailed from Florida to Portugal and Spain. My second favorite activity outside of work is reading. I was able to read five books on the cruise!
