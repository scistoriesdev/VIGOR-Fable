Personal Introduction

Hello! My name is Jane, and I am a Clinical Research Coordinator in the Dermatology department here at UMass Chan. As a Coordinator, I’m the liason between patients and investigators. I also work on regulatory tasks and perform study visits. VIGOR is just one of the many studies I am part of, and I’m grateful that I get to work with such an amazing team and be part of cutting-edge research in the field of science!

Your Background and Journey

I graduated in May 2023 with a Bachelor of Science in Public Health, and minor in Sociology. An accomplishment in my career journey so far is when I worked with the Medical Reserve Corps and wrote a 200+ page comprehensive community assessment on 22 towns in Massachusetts. I had the opportunity to travel through all these towns, and interview various opinion leaders such as directors of public health and local public health nurses to get a better sense of the needs of each community. I’m new to the field of research, and for now, I plan to continue working in this field because I enjoy being at the forefront of advancing knowledge and discovery. I’ve found research to be meaningful because we are pushing the boundaries of what is known and striving to make meaningful contributions to our understanding of the world.

Outside of Work

It’s difficult to talk about a particular hobby I have outside of work because I tend to jump between many interests! A few things I enjoy are: playing music (piano, ukulele), baking, tennis, scrapbooking and going to the gym. I’m also a sucker for binge-watching a good tv series- a fun fact is I finished watching all 11 seasons of Modern Family in one summer! Additionally, as a first-generation Lao, I’m passionate about representing my background and culture while actively contributing to the progress of Southeast Asians. In college, my friends and I created an Asian cultural club to celebrate and acknowledge different Asian cultures on campus. The club is still thriving to this day, and it’s my hope that in the future, we can pave the way for a brighter and more inclusive future for Southeast Asians.
