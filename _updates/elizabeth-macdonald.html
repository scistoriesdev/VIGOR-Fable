Personal Introduction

I am a clinical research coordinator in the Department of Dermatology at UMass Chan. I conduct study visits, interviews, and tests, while maintaining regulatory documentation to support physician investigators. My research primarily focuses on vitiligo, but my work also includes other diseases such as epidermolysis bullosa, atopic dermatitis, and lupus.

Your Background and Journey

As a biology major graduating amidst the pandemic, I was excited to be involved in the medical field to make an impact. I applied the skills I learned from my lab experience in college to process COVID-19 PCR nose swab tests during the peak of the pandemic. Once testing decreased, I sought a position as a clinical research coordinator where I could spend my time working with patients and conducting research trials. Over the past two years, I have worked with Dr. Harris on numerous vitiligo studies where I have learned about the important role of research in medicine. While at UMass Chan, I am pursuing a master’s degree in public health to learn more about the role of a physician on a population level. I am especially excited by our VIGOR study because we are seeking to understand why people develop vitiligo and the risk factors between genes and the environment on a population level. I most look forward to connecting and working with patients throughout the project.

Outside of Work

Outside of work I spend time volunteering with my local health department hosting health information sessions and vaccine clinics. Additionally, I enjoy traveling and cooking, specifically pasta dishes. I am currently planning a trip to Italy this summer to enjoy what I hope will be the best pasta!
