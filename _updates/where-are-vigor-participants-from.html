It’s hard to believe it, but we are now 8 months into the study with actively enrolled participants. We wanted to provide you with an update on our progress with the study and give you a heads up about what to expect over the next couple of months. There is a lot to cover, so let’s get to it!

Enrollment Update

As of late January, 2025, we have 585 participants from 183 families spread across 42 states. This means that we’re over half-way to building our full cohort of 1000 participants! As you can see from the map, there are regions where we’ll be especially focused in our recruiting efforts, for example in the Gulf Coast and Southeastern states.

We’d love your help in our recruiting efforts. Here’s what you can do:

  • Sign up family members! If you have close family members that haven’t joined the study yet, contact us at vigor@umassmed.edu and we’ll help you get them signed up. The upcoming holidays are a great time to talk with family about joining the study.

Webinar Recap

On Tuesday, January 14th we held our most recent webinar with Drs. John Harris and Manuel Garber where they provided an update on the study and answered participant questions. They also recapped the winners from our first raffle for participants which was drawn on December 3, 2024. 50 winners were selected for prizes ranging from $50 to $500 and totaling almost $5,000. Our next raffle will happen in June 2025, and to maximize your chances of winning, be sure to complete any unfinished surveys in the VIGOR app. Tickets are awarded based on completion of study activities, so be sure to keep up to date on study tasks!

During the webinar, Drs. Harris and Garber also made another exciting announcement: all study participants will be receiving a FitBit watch! These will be mailed out later this spring, so be on the lookout for more information about this exciting addition to the VIGOR study. If you aren’t yet a part of the VIGOR study, this is just another great reason to join.


If you missed the webinar or would like to rewatch it, you can find it (along with all previous webinars) on our webinars page.
