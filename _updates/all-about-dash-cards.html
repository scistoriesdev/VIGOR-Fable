From time to time, individuals in the VIGOR study may earn financial compensation for their participation. For example, the "Get a Coffee on Us" survey and the bi-annual raffles are two examples of how study participants can earn compensation in the VIGOR study. To make the delivery of earnings simple, our team uses digital prepaid Visa cards delivered through a program called DASH. To receive your DASH card, participants must ensure they have their email, phone number, and home address entered in their profile in the MyDataHelps app because the VIGOR team needs this information to issue the card.

When money has been earned, participants will receive an email from UMass Chan Medical School with instructions on how to claim and register to use the card. Here is an example of what that email looks like:

After following the steps in the email, a participant will have successfully claimed their card. Any future compensation will be reloaded onto this same card. The MyDashCard mobile app can be downloaded to manage the digital card. Although the card may have an expiration date, the funds on cards do not expire. Upon expiration, you must contact customer service (Corporate@in-prepaid.com) to access the remaining balance on a card. Please also note that there are certain fees associated with using the DASH card.

Finally, any participants receiving $300 or more from a single study within a single calendar year at UMass Chan will need to provide their social security number and complete a W-9 form (tax form). The study team will reach out to those participants who meet this criteria.
