Your Background and Journey

I graduated from a small medical school in a rural town in Brazil, where the curriculum focused heavily on internal medicine and offered little exposure to dermatology. Naturally, I pursued internal medicine and later trained as a critical care physician. In the ICU, I encountered patients with severe dermatologic conditions—one case of Stevens-Johnson Syndrome in particular left a lasting impression. That experience opened my eyes to the unique role of dermatology in even the most critical settings. I became fascinated by the visual language of the skin: patterns, colors, primary and secondary lesions—all clues that guide diagnosis and treatment.

Over time, I became especially drawn to pigmentary disorders, and in particular, vitiligo captured my curiosity and empathy. The science is fascinating, but what moved me most were the stories—the emotional weight carried by those living with vitiligo, and the life-changing power of even small areas of repigmentation. My passion for this field continues to grow as I contribute to research that aims to improve both treatments and understanding of the disease.

My years in the ICU also taught me to remain calm in high-pressure situations, but also to connect deeply with patients in moments of vulnerability. I’m not afraid to take on challenging cases or make tough decisions—and these skills have become the foundation of my approach to dermatology.

Over the past year, I have had the honor of being the Skin of Color Society Research Fellow at UMass Chan Medical School. This experience has been transformative—it’s my first true immersion in U.S.-based clinical dermatology and research and it has only deepened my commitment to this specialty. In the VIGOR Study, I am excited to work with a collaborative team dedicated to managing recruitment, enhancing the participant experience, and handling project logistics.

Outside of Work

Beyond the hospital and research lab, I am also a creative and sensitive soul. Even while working full-time in medicine, I have always chased beauty and creativity. Styling floral arrangements, curating elegant gatherings, designing layered, soulful interiors—these are the ways I recharge. I believe in the power of beautiful, mindful spaces and in creating moments that bring people together and linger long after the event ends.

That passion came to life with The Watson Boutique Bed & Breakfast, a boutique inn that I founded and designed from the ground up. Located in a historic New England home from 1767, it’s more than a place to stay—it’s an experience. I created the concept, curated every detail of the design, and remain responsible for its day-to-day operations. Through The Watson, I’ve found a way to translate my love for aesthetics, storytelling, and hospitality into a welcoming, immersive space.

I’m an artistic dreamer with an eye for detail, passionate about textures, vintage finds, and the harmony of contrast. My aesthetic blends old and new, softness and boldness, simplicity and depth. Whether it’s a well-set table or a carefully designed room, I love turning simple ideas into long-lasting experiences. At the intersection of science and soul, clinical precision and creative expression—that’s where I feel most alive.
