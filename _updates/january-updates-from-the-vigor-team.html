FITBIT WATCHES

More Options Coming Your Way

Fitbits are still our main device — and we’re aiming to get one to every participant as soon as possible. We’re also expanding support for additional wearable devices. If you'd like to use your own device (Apple Watch, Garmin, etc.) instead of, or alongside, the Fitbit, contact us at vigor@umassmed.edu for instructions.

Confirm Your Address to Receive Your Fitbit

To receive your Fitbit, please complete the Fitbit consent and confirm your shipping address. You should have received an email and in-app notification. Find it in your MyDataHelps main page. We cannot ship your device until this step is completed. Once confirmed, your watch will be sent!

PHOTOS UPLOAD

Photo Upload Improvements are coming soon.

Some participants have had issues uploading multiple photos to report vitiligo changes. A full app upgrade is coming this month that will make photo uploads much smoother.
