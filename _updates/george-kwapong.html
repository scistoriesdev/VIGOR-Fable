Personal Introduction

I am a 2023 MBBS graduate from Wenzhou Medical University in Zhejiang, China. I recently heard about the world of clinical research from my cousin who is an International Medical Graduate like me. After I returned from a required year of internship during my medical training, I learned about clinical research through the Clinical Research Fastrack which helped me understand the ins and outs of clinical research and how important it is.

Your Background and Journey

I was born and raised in Ghana, West Africa, and developed an interest in healthcare from an early age. I was not a child that was often in the hospital, yet I still hated going there – the injections, the smell of alcohol pads, everything. As I got older, I started to appreciate the work that physicians did, and I started wanting to help people feel better the same way they helped me feel better. I’ve been working towards the goal of becoming a physician since I was a teenager and as I alluded to earlier, I wasn’t the only one in my family that pursued medicine. In addition to my cousin, my older brother is also involved in healthcare and research, specifically in neurology and ophthalmology. One day I hope to collaborate with him!

Outside of Work

Outside of work, I really enjoy playing music. I have played the bass guitar for about 10 years and I still play at my church every week. While schooling in China, I met a group of guys who loved music just like me and we played together often. One of my favorite shows was when we performed for the President of Ghana, Nana Akuffo Addo, during his visit to Beijing. We were invited to play some Ghanaian classic songs for him an official function during that trip.
