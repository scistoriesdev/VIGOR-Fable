Study overview

Unlocking the secrets of vitiligo

VIGOR stands for Vitiligo Genetics of Onset and Relapse. Over five years we are following 1,000 people to learn why vitiligo begins, why it comes back after treatment, and which genes and environmental triggers set it off.

We are not testing a treatment. Everything happens from home, through the VIGOR app and kits we mail to you. What we learn should help doctors predict who is at risk and stop vitiligo before it starts.

Who can join?

  • People with vitiligo, age 6 or older, living anywhere in the US or Canada.
  • Their parents, siblings, children and cousins who do not have vitiligo.

We need more family members without vitiligo than people with it.

To tell which genes matter, we compare relatives who share DNA but not the condition. If you have vitiligo, the most useful thing you can do is invite your family.

What’s involved?

At the start
Enroll in the VIGOR app and mail back one saliva sample.
Every 3 months
A short survey in the app, about 10 minutes.
Every 6 months
Skin and blood samples you collect at home with a mailed kit and return by prepaid post.
For 5 years
No clinic visits, ever. Leave at any time.
