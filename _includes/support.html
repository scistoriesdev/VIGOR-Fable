Funding & Support

Support from the National Institutes of Health, private foundations, philanthropists, and other partners has made this work possible. With continued NIH funding, VIGOR takes the next step by studying how vitiligo begins in the first place. By better understanding its root causes, we hope to uncover new opportunities to treat vitiligo more effectively, work toward a cure, and one day help prevent it from developing.
